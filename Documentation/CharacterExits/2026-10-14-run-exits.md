# Character run-ending animations

When health reaches zero and no revival remains, the four playable characters now leave the fight through a nonlethal animation. The red particle/wipe sequence is replaced. The existing failure panel and run reward saving still run through `Character.OnDeath`, once the final animation pose and hold have finished.

| Character | Exit | Approximate duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ashi | Shoulders slump, sigh, reluctantly wave a white surrender flag, lower the arm | 3 seconds |
| Ari | Frightened crouch, transform into the existing cream/gold marble phoenix, flap upward to safety | 2.4 seconds |
| Hyuki | Fold hands over chest while standing straight, freeze from the shoes upward inside translucent faceted ice | 2.7 seconds |
| Shini | Gather energy, transform the body into a living flame, rise and disappear as embers | 2.4 seconds |

Hyuki's reference is **Level 1 `Hyuki_ColdIdle` / `Hyuki_Walk`**, including the narrow eyes, gray trousers and white shoes. It is not the round main-menu portrait. The ice follows the existing `PhoenixSkills/IcePrison` appearance.

## Playback and sizing

- At terminal HP0, cancel the hit flash and active dash, stop abilities/held-weapon visuals, return lingering player projectiles to their pools, clear death/dust particles and stop the player's physics body. Further damage, healing, movement and skill input cannot restart the actor.
- Pause combat and play the exit with unscaled time. Early `GameOver` calls cannot skip it, and a simultaneous boss-clear callback cannot replace a terminal defeat with victory.
- Revival still runs before this path, restoring health with its existing invulnerability.
- Calibrate each clip once from the current character's idle sprite mesh height and world scale. Every frame uses that same scale and ground anchor; large wings/ice/flags do not cause per-frame auto-fitting.
- Ashi and Hyuki retain their final poses. Ari flies upward and Shini dissolves into flame; their ground shadows fade with the exit and disappear completely. The result panel opens after the final hold.
- Atlases use transparent, point-filtered, uncompressed PNG textures. JSON stores frame order, duration and reference body height. No shared-link deployment or mobile asset conversion is part of this change.

## Art provenance

Built-in imagegen produced the animation frames from existing project character art. The approved Ashi/Ari/Shini designs were retained; Hyuki was regenerated using the gameplay references above. Frames were sliced, ground-aligned and packed without per-frame rescaling. Ashi's flag poses that switched hands were excluded from playback.

References: `AshiRemake/Ashi_Idle_8_Eyebrows.png`, `CharacterDesigns/Ari_Idle.png`, `AriSkills/Marble/Idle0.png`, `CharacterDesigns/Hyuki_ColdIdle.png`, `CharacterDesigns/Hyuki_Walk.png`, `PhoenixSkills/IcePrison.png`, `CharacterDesigns/Shini_Idle.png` under `Assets/Junhan/Art`.

## Verification

`Tools > Character exits > Import and verify all four in Level 1`, or Unity batch mode with `-executeMethod Vampire.Editor.CharacterExitChecks.Run`.

The check runs the real Level 1 for each character, checks revival first, applies lethal damage, verifies the panel is absent throughout the unscaled animation, blocks early result/clear calls, checks repeated damage/healing/skill input, verifies fixed player/clip scale and position, and confirms exactly one final failure event. It also checks held-weapon hiding and disappearing-character shadow cleanup. Ashi is tested during a dash and Ari during phoenix transformation. Temporary test currency/tutorial flags are backed up and restored.

Evidence: `Proof/checks.txt`, `Proof/result.txt`, and the per-character before/result screenshots. The local full capture series and logs are under `Library/CharacterExitProof`.
